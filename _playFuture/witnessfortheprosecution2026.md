London, 1926. When Leonard Vole, a charming young man, is accused of murdering a
wealthy spinster, he finds himself at the centre of a sensational murder trial.

Despite protesting his innocence, the evidence against him appears compelling,
and his fate rests in the hands of a brilliant defence barrister determined to
secure his acquittal.

With his devoted wife Romaine by his side, Leonard must fight to clear his name
before it’s too late.

As the trial unfolds, the tension mounts and the truth becomes ever more
elusive. Will justice prevail, or will an innocent man be sent to the gallows?
